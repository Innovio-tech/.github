# Code of conduct

Everyone who works in Innovio repositories, inside the company or outside it, is expected to:

- be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews and chat; criticise code and decisions, not people;
- assume good intent, and ask before concluding;
- keep personal data, credentials and customer information out of public places;
- not harass, insult or discriminate against anyone, for any reason.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the repository's maintainers or the Innovio management. Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that break this code and may restrict
the access of someone who keeps breaking it.

This code follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
